General Instructions

  1. The total duration of the examination is . The countdown timer at the top right of the screen shows the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the examination ends by itself and your answers are submitted automatically.
  2. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question:
    1 You have not visited the question yet.2 You have not answered the question.3 You have answered the question.4 Not answered, but marked for review.5 Answered and marked for review (will be evaluated).
  3. Click a question number in the palette to go to that question directly. This does NOT save the answer to the current question.
  4. Click Save & Next to save your answer and go to the next question. Click Mark for Review & Next to save and mark it for review. Click Clear Response to deselect your answer.
  5. For numerical value questions, enter the answer using the on-screen keypad (or keyboard). Enter the value truncated/rounded off to two decimal places where required.
  6. Answers to questions marked for review are also evaluated.
  7. The test runs in one sitting. There is no pause. The clock keeps running if you switch apps, leave full screen, lock the screen, or close the page. If the page closes by accident, open the paper again to continue with the time that is left.
  8. The exam opens in full screen. Every time you leave the exam window it is recorded, and the count and time away appear in your report.
